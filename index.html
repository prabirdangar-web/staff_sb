2. Promotion / Career Advancement / Higher Scale Movement

Career chain (AT → Higher Scale → AHM → HM): add events in date order; each is fixed on top of the previous one. ROPA-09: AHM / HM of Junior High School = one additional increment in old scale, then GP = AT's GP + AGP Rs. 200 (e.g. 4800 → 5000); target GP is automatic. HM High School = GP 5400; HM Higher Secondary = GP 5400 + AGP Rs. 200. AHM → HM: the AHM stage is ignored for the HM fixation — pay is first re-fitted as AT (background) on the date preceding HM appointment, then HM fixation is applied (one addl. increment in old scale, GP 5400 from AT's GP, not from AHM's GP). ROPA-19: no AGP — AGP Rs. 200 is deducted before fitment, AHM/HM(Jr) keep the same Level, HM (High / Higher Sec.) = Level 16. Next increment date follows the ROPA in force (ROPA-98: promotion-month anniversary; ROPA-09/19: 1st July). AHM / HM of Junior High School (no change of scale): date of next increment continues as per AT. Same-GP event = designation change only. Reversion to AT: GP/Level go back to AT; pay already drawn is protected (not reduced) — the higher of the protected pay and the pay AT would have drawn is fixed. Higher Scale events are kept; target GP is automatic.
Fixation Rule Settings: Functional post promotions grant an extra increment before matching the target scale. Higher scale movements for qualification/upgradations bypass the promotional increment step. For HM/AHM (Level-16), one increment in the existing level is always added before fitment. If joining as HM/AHM is between 2nd January and 30th June, the option is exercised w.e.f. 1st July of that year; the pay table shows ONE row from 1st July to 30th June of the next year (the 1st July increment is included in that row, not repeated), and the Remarks column states the actual date of appointment; the next increment is 1st July of the following year. If the joining date is outside 2nd January–30th June, no same-year 1st July option row is created and fixation remains on the actual joining date.
